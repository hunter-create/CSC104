Hi, my name is Hunter, and I’m a photographer based in Arizona. Every photo you see on this website is one I’ve personally taken, spending countless hours perfecting and editing each image. Many of these photos started as ideas—concepts I envisioned and brought to life by setting up scenes and collaborating with the people around me. I make it a priority to recognize those who contribute to my work, whether they are subjects, assistants, or sources of inspiration.

If you’re curious about why I chose a particular image or who the subject is, I love sharing the stories behind my work.The first few photos you see on this site are especially meaningful to me—they feature my girlfriend, who joined me on an adventure to a beautiful place with scenic trails and abundant wildlife. While I originally set out to capture the landscape, I couldn’t help but make her the focus of many shots.I hope you enjoy these images and appreciate the time, passion, and effort that went into each one.
